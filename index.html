Who We Are

Infrastructure for inclusive commerce.

SwiftTap One is a Botswana-based fintech building a modular, cloud-hosted digital payments platform for the Bank of Botswana Regulatory Sandbox. We are not building another app — we are building the rails for inclusive, interoperable commerce.

Our platform enables consumers to pay via NFC tap, QR code, payment link, or peer-to-peer transfer. Merchants of any scale — from formal retail to informal trade — accept digital payments with the smartphone they already own.

We operate under direct Bank of Botswana supervision as a sandbox participant, with AML/CFT, consumer protection, ICT security, and risk management frameworks governing every operation.
