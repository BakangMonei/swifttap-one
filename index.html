Who We Are

Built to bridge the
gap in digital payments.

SwiftTap One is a Botswana-based fintech innovation developing a modular, cloud-hosted digital payments platform purpose-built for the Bank of Botswana's Regulatory Sandbox. We are not simply building a payments app — we are building the infrastructure for inclusive commerce.

Our platform enables consumers to pay using NFC tap, QR code, payment link, or peer-to-peer transfer, and enables merchants of all sizes — from formal retailers to informal traders — to accept digital payments using only the smartphone they already own.

We operate under the direct supervision of the Bank of Botswana as a sandbox participant, with a full suite of AML/CFT, consumer protection, ICT security, and risk management frameworks governing every aspect of our operations.

Regulatory Sandbox

Supervised. Limited. Safe.

SwiftTap One's sandbox participation is designed to be exactly what the Bank of Botswana expects: controlled, low-exposure, consumer-safe, and built around measurable outcomes.

Every consumer who joins the sandbox is protected. Every transaction is monitored. Every incident is reported. Every peso of consumer funds is held in a legally ring-fenced custodian account — not a SwiftTap One asset under any circumstances.

We are not asking regulators to trust us on faith. We are showing them our controls, our evidence, and our commitment — document by document.

Our Foundations

What we stand on.

Six pillars underpin everything SwiftTap One does — from the first line of code to the last regulatory report.

🛡️
Consumer Protection First
Every design decision starts with one question: does this protect the consumer? Zero fees, zero hidden charges, full fund segregation, and unconditional complaint rights.
🔐
Security by Design
Zero Trust architecture. AES-256 encryption everywhere. Biometric MFA. Immutable audit logs. Maker-checker on every sensitive action. Security is not a feature — it is the foundation.
⚖️
Full Regulatory Compliance
AML/CFT/CPF framework aligned to FATF and the Financial Intelligence Act. BDPA 2018 data protection. Bank of Botswana sandbox conditions. All 12 compliance frameworks fully documented.
🌍
Financial Inclusion Mission
30% of pilot participants drawn from unbanked or underbanked segments. Setswana language support. Assisted onboarding. Vulnerable customer policy. Inclusion is not a metric — it is our mandate.
🔄
Interoperability
OpenAPI 3.0 standards. ISO 20022 compatible settlement. Partner-agnostic architecture. Designed to work with any Botswana bank, any mobile money provider, and the BoB monitoring environment.
📊
Supervisory Transparency
Real-time KPI feeds to the Bank of Botswana. Incident notification within 2 hours. Audit log access within 24 hours. Nothing happens in SwiftTap One that the regulator cannot see.
